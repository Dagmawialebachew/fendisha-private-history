# Voice Guide

Do **not** perform these like a speech. Record them on your phone somewhere quiet. Leave small pauses, laughs and imperfect wording. That is better.

Put files in `public/media/voice/` with these exact filenames.

## `01-opening.mp3` — 15–25 sec

Idea, not a script:

“hey fkr… okay first, happy birthday yene Fendisha 😂💜. headphones dergish if you have them… and don’t rush through this eshi? just follow the buttons. I made this for you.”

## `02-calls.mp3` — 20–35 sec

Talk about the two-hour calls naturally:

“sometimes I don’t even remember what the hell we talked about for that long 😂. the call would literally end and we would just call again. I think I loved how normal it felt… just having you there.”

## `03-distance.mp3` — 35–60 sec

Keep it warm, not guilty:

- when you need space you go quiet
- in your head that does not mean you stopped loving her
- she only gets what you show her
- you are learning to communicate that better
- end with “I miss you, fkr.”

## `04-faith.mp3` — 60–120 sec

This one should be fully real.

Tell her:

- what you thought when she told you about the man from church
- that you did not want fear of losing her to turn into selfishness
- talking to friends did not fully settle you
- going to ቅዳሴ
- the real prayer/thought: “if she is for me… keep her”

Do not add anything spiritual that you did not actually feel or pray.

## `05-future.mp3` — 35–60 sec

“I don’t know exactly what our future looks like… I’m not gonna fake certainty just because it’s your birthday. gn when I think about my future, you show up in it a lot. I wanna see more life with you.”

Mention her own growth too, not only marriage/kids.

## `06-final-birthday.mp3` — 2–4 min

This is the big one. Do not read polished prose.

Touch these points:

- happy 21st birthday
- how wholeheartedly she loves
- how comfortable she makes you
- her support for your dreams
- her laugh / why Fendisha exists
- what good things you want for her personally
- that you love who she is now and want to see who she becomes
- easy us / difficult us / far us / laughing us / praying us
- “after all of that… fkr, I still choose you.”
- finish in your real mixed Amharic-English voice

Suggested last feeling, not mandatory wording:

“happy birthday yene Fendisha… lafendadash ende 😂❤️”
